    What's real and what's made up

    Real: Sun and Moon positions, from low-precision formulas. For Kongsberg on 5 Nov they agree with meteoblue's page to within 3 minutes (sunset 16:17 vs 16:19, moonrise 02:52 vs 02:50, moonset 14:59, phase 14%).

    Made up: every cloud, seeing and wind value, the four model names and the two regional domains. Model D shows no seeing, to show how a model with too few pressure levels would look. Time zones are approximated from longitude. Seeing carries the "uncalibrated" label it will carry until the historical track's calibration exists.

    Seeing is never blended between hours or grid cells here: the globe steps hour by hour and shows each 2° or 3° cell as a block.
